# Memory Garden illustration assets

Created for this project with the built-in imagegen tool. These illustrations are fictional botanical artwork, not depictions of a patient's memories. Original generated files were copied into `public/garden/`; alpha was preserved for plant cutouts. Growth stages are rendered in native SVG/CSS and do not modify the PNGs.

## Files

- `public/garden/garden-background.png` — watercolor garden background.
- `public/garden/daisy.png` — white marguerite daisy cutout.
- `public/garden/gardenia.png` — ivory gardenia cutout.
- `public/garden/calendula.png` — warm gold calendula cutout.

## Final background prompt

Use case: illustration. Asset type: landscape background for Memory Garden, a calm reminiscence companion for adults. Create a sophisticated hand painted watercolor storybook garden panorama, 16:9 landscape, fine cold press paper grain, delicate botanical details, creamy morning light, soft sage green grasses, misty blue and lavender distant trees, pale ivory sky. A meandering pale sandy path from lower center toward a very distant understated wooden garden gate near right third, loose lush borders of white daisies, pale pink garden roses, a few soft golden calendula and blue delphiniums along the far left and right edges. Gentle atmospheric depth and watercolor pigment blooms, natural and dignified, botanical illustration rather than cartoon. The center foreground (middle 40% of frame, lower half) must be OPEN, low pale grass and light earth, leaving visual room for a separate interactive growing flower. Upper half should be very pale almost blank morning mist for UI readability. No people, faces, animals, lettering, UI, buildings, logos or watermarks. Avoid photorealism, harsh shadows, saturated colors, dense visual clutter. Full bleed illustrated landscape with softly faded off white upper edges.

## Final plant prompt template

Use case: illustration. Asset type: transparent botanical plant cutout for the Memory Garden app. [SUBJECT] Refined hand-painted watercolor botanical illustration on truly transparent background. Visible subtle watercolor pigment and fine petal texture, natural adult-oriented botanical atlas aesthetic. Muted warm palette, soft edges but clear plant silhouette. Vertical 2:3 composition, entire plant fully inside frame with small margins, plant fills 85% of frame height. Natural curved stem, no planter, pot, ground, paper rectangle, text, faces, shadows, symbols, logo or watermark. Isolate the complete ONE plant on transparent background. Match a quiet watercolor morning garden.

Subjects:

1. A single elegant white marguerite daisy plant, warm golden central disk, one fully open white daisy facing slightly upward in the upper center, one small unopened bud, fine green stem with two delicate leaves, entire plant from blossom down to tiny root base.
2. A single elegant gardenia plant, one fully open layered ivory gardenia blossom in the upper center, one small unopened bud, slender green stem with two dark sage oval leaves, entire plant from blossom down to tiny root base.
3. A single elegant calendula plant, one fully open soft warm gold calendula flower in the upper center, one small unopened bud, slender green stem with two narrow leaves, entire plant from blossom down to tiny root base.
